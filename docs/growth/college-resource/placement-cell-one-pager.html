First Offer Reality Guide — one-page summary

A free PDF resource for outgoing students, covering what their CTC actually becomes once PF, gratuity, insurance, and tax are accounted for. No cost, no signup required for students, no promotional content beyond a link to free calculators.

Covers

CTC vs. gross vs. in-hand, variable pay, PF, gratuity, tax regime choice, offer-comparison checklist, questions to ask HR.

Format

4-page PDF, plus a free web/embed version for your placement-resource page.

Grounded in

SalaryExit India's FY 2026-27 tax/PF calculation engine — same engine behind the site's public calculators.

Not

Tax filing, legal, or payroll advice. Explicitly stated as educational content throughout.

To use: link the PDF or the embeddable table from your placement-resource page, include an excerpt in a placement-cell newsletter, or request a short campus session for outgoing students.
