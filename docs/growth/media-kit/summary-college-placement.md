# College placement-cell summary: SalaryExit India In-Hand Salary Model 2026

**For:** Training & Placement Officers (TPOs), placement-cell resource pages, pre-placement talks.

**The problem this solves for your students:** most students accept their first offer letter without understanding that the CTC number on the letter is not what lands in their bank account. This causes real financial-planning mistakes in the first year of a job (unrealistic rent budgets, no emergency fund, surprise TDS).

**What's useful for a placement resource page or PPT slide:**
- A simple table showing, at common fresher CTC bands (₹5L–₹10L), what the *real* monthly in-hand looks like — not a rule of thumb, a modeled number from a live tax engine.
- The "required CTC for a target in-hand" table, useful for students negotiating or comparing multiple offers by take-home rather than headline CTC.
- Free, no signup, no login, no ads targeting students, no cost to the institution.
