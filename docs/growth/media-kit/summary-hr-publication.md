# HR-publication summary: SalaryExit India In-Hand Salary Model 2026

**For:** HR/payroll/compensation publications, HRMS blogs, employee-benefits content.

**Why this matters to your audience:** "Why doesn't my in-hand match my CTC?" is one of the most common employee support tickets HR and payroll teams field, especially right after onboarding or a raise. This model quantifies exactly where the gap comes from and how big it typically is.

**What the data shows:**
- Employer-cost structure (how much of CTC goes to PF/gratuity/insurance vs. fixed cash) alone can shift monthly in-hand by thousands of rupees at the *same* CTC — useful context when explaining offer-letter structuring to candidates.
- Capped-PF vs. full-Basic-PF elections have a modeled, quantified monthly cash trade-off against retirement corpus — useful for benefits communication.
- A reverse-calculated table shows the CTC range required to guarantee a target monthly in-hand (₹50k/₹75k/₹1L/₹1.5L/₹2L) — useful for recruiter/compensation conversations with candidates who think in take-home terms.
