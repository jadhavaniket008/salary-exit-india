# Launch visual assets — required-ctc-report-2026

5 factual chart families, each rendered at 1200×630 (social) and 1080×1350 (LinkedIn/Reddit vertical), plus a matching CSV slice. All generated by `scripts/growth/generate-launch-visuals.ts` from `lib/growth/chart-image.tsx`, which reads live from `generateInHandSalaryModel()` — the exact same data module the report page and downloads use. **No value here is hardcoded separately from the model.**

**Design system:** reused, not reinvented — the palette (`#FAFAF8` background, `#1A6B45` "Salary Green" accent, Geist Sans) and brand line are the same ones already documented for this exact launch in `brag-output/brag-plan.md`'s "Visual identity" section from earlier in this engagement, kept consistent with the site's own established look rather than introducing a new one for these assets specifically.

Stored in `public/growth-assets/required-ctc-report-2026/`.

## 1. `in-hand-by-ctc` — ₹5–50 LPA estimated monthly in-hand

- **Files:** `in-hand-by-ctc-1200x630.png`, `in-hand-by-ctc-1080x1350.png`, `in-hand-by-ctc.csv`
- **Accessible text summary:** A horizontal bar chart showing estimated monthly in-hand salary for 12 CTC levels from ₹5 lakh to ₹50 lakh per year, at a 13% employer-cost structure with statutory-ceiling PF, under the new tax regime. Monthly in-hand rises from roughly ₹35,400 at ₹5L CTC to approximately ₹3,58,300 at ₹50L CTC — not linearly, because tax takes a larger share at higher incomes.
- **Source/methodology line:** "Modelled from SalaryExit's FY 2026-27 tax/PF engine — not a survey." (matches the chart's own footer)
- **CSV columns:** `annual_ctc, monthly_in_hand`

## 2. `required-ctc` — required CTC for a target monthly in-hand

- **Files:** `required-ctc-1200x630.png`, `required-ctc-1080x1350.png`, `required-ctc.csv`
- **Accessible text summary:** A horizontal bar chart showing the CTC required (point estimate, 10% employer-cost structure) to reach five common monthly in-hand targets: ₹50,000, ₹75,000, ₹1,00,000, ₹1,50,000, and ₹2,00,000. Required CTC ranges from roughly ₹6.6L for a ₹50k/month target to approximately ₹25.7L for a ₹2L/month target. The chart notes that the actual required CTC varies further (±) depending on employer-cost structure — the full low/high range is in the flagship report and in this image's own CSV slice's sibling, `salaryexit-in-hand-model-2026.csv`.
- **Source/methodology line:** "Actual required CTC ranges ±depending on employer-cost structure — see full report for low/high."
- **CSV columns:** `target_monthly_in_hand, required_ctc_low, required_ctc_point, required_ctc_high`

## 3. `pf-comparison` — capped PF vs. full-Basic PF

- **Files:** `pf-comparison-1200x630.png`, `pf-comparison-1080x1350.png`, `pf-comparison.csv`
- **Accessible text summary:** A horizontal bar chart pairing capped-PF and full-Basic-PF monthly in-hand figures at five CTC levels (₹10L, ₹15L, ₹20L, ₹25L, ₹30L), all at a 13% employer-cost structure. At every level, full-Basic PF produces lower monthly in-hand — e.g. at ₹20L CTC, ₹1,31,465/month (capped) vs. ₹1,25,435/month (full-Basic), a difference of roughly ₹6,030/month for a larger PF corpus.
- **Source/methodology line:** "Full-Basic PF means lower monthly cash, larger retirement corpus."
- **CSV columns:** `annual_ctc, capped_pf_monthly_in_hand, full_basic_pf_monthly_in_hand, monthly_difference`

## 4. `employer-cost-scenarios` — same ₹20L CTC, three employer-cost structures

- **Files:** `employer-cost-scenarios-1200x630.png`, `employer-cost-scenarios-1080x1350.png`, `employer-cost-scenarios.csv`
- **Accessible text summary:** A horizontal bar chart showing monthly in-hand for an identical ₹20L CTC under three employer-cost assumptions: 8% (lean structure), 13% (typical, highlighted), and 18% (heavy structure). Monthly in-hand is highest under the 8% scenario and lowest under 18% — the entire difference comes from how much of the CTC is employer-side cost (PF, gratuity, insurance) versus fixed cash gross, not from any difference in the headline CTC.
- **Source/methodology line:** "8% and 18% represent lean vs. heavy employer-cost structures."
- **CSV columns:** `employer_cost_share_pct, monthly_in_hand`

## 5. `waterfall` — ₹18L CTC: where the money goes

- **Files:** `waterfall-1200x630.png`, `waterfall-1080x1350.png`, `waterfall.csv`
- **Accessible text summary:** A three-segment horizontal bar (waterfall-style) breaking a ₹18L CTC down into: employer costs (~₹19,500/month — company spend that never reaches the employee), PF + tax deductions (~₹10,991/month), and monthly in-hand (~₹1,19,509/month). This is the same ₹18L worked example used elsewhere in SalaryExit's calculators (`CTC_WORKED_EXAMPLE_INPUT`), kept consistent across the product rather than picking a new example for this asset.
- **Source/methodology line:** "Same worked example used throughout SalaryExit's calculators."
- **CSV columns:** `segment, monthly_value`

## Requirements checklist

- ✅ Readable on mobile — verified visually at both 1200×630 and the taller 1080×1350 vertical format (screenshots reviewed during generation).
- ✅ No decorative clutter — flat bars, no gradients/shadows/icons, consistent with the "no over-decoration" constraint carried through this entire engagement.
- ✅ No false personalized claim — every figure is a modeled scenario explicitly labeled with its assumptions (CTC level, employer-cost %, PF scenario), never presented as "your" number.
- ✅ No hardcoded value separate from the generated model — `lib/growth/chart-image.tsx` reads every number from `generateInHandSalaryModel()`, the same function the report page and CSV/JSON downloads use.
- ✅ No sensational phrasing — labels are plain ("estimated monthly in-hand," "required CTC," "the real cash trade-off," not "shocking," "secret," etc.).
- ✅ SalaryExit branding visible but secondary — small accent-colored wordmark in the footer, not a dominant logo lockup.
