## One correction made during this audit (see git history for the exact diff)

**"Section 87A rebate cliff" language was inaccurate and has been corrected.** The new-regime marginal-relief formula (`rebate87ANewRegime` in `lib/calculators/income-tax.ts`) does not produce a discontinuous jump — it caps tax-after-rebate at exactly `taxableIncome − ₹12,00,000` until slab tax naturally exceeds that cap. Numerically verified (binary search against the real engine functions, see `findMarginalReliefZoneEndTaxableIncome()`): from ₹12,00,000 to ₹12,70,587 taxable income, the **marginal rate is exactly 100%** (every extra rupee taxed away in full) — a real, precise, engine-derived effect, but not a "cliff" in the sense of jumping straight to full slab tax. The original finding also implicitly conflated the ₹12L CTC band with the ₹12L *taxable-income* threshold, which are different bases (at 13% employer cost, ₹12L CTC → ₹9.69L taxable income; the ₹12L taxable-income threshold is actually crossed around ₹14.66L CTC). Both the language and the CTC/taxable-income conflation are now fixed in `lib/growth/in-hand-salary-model.ts`, and `public/data/salaryexit-in-hand-model-2026.{csv,json}` were regenerated. A second, related bug was also caught and fixed: the stale committed data files still contained an old "FY FY2026-27" double-label typo from an earlier session that had been fixed in source but never re-generated — now regenerated and consistent.
